NO LONGER RELEVANT
new images will come from Matricula website, not the archive printing system.


This image shows printout from an archive-image in _TABLE-DOUBLE format.

This example shows entries printed in _LANDSCAPE mode.
(The printout headers are always printed in portrait mode.)

The example shows three non-adjacent entries copied from the one archive-image.

Each entry line on the archive-image was captured using 2 regions.  
Note that there is a small overlap between the left and right hand captures to
prove that they go together.


Note that most of the printout area is used.
If the entry lines were not captured with 2 regions, each would need
to be pasted on the A4 area at a reduced size so as to fit the length.
That would make them 1/2 as wide, so the final page would be half empty. That
wastes resolution.


Note that if adjacent entries were desired (say #3 and #4), then they would 
be captured as a block. First the 3 and 4 lines left side, then the 3 and 4 line right side.
There would still be enough space to capture another non-adjacent entry.
