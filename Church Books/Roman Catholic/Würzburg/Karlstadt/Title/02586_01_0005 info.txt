Register
of the Baptized
of the Parish
of Karlstadt,
begun
by
Jacobus Göllner,
a native of Hassfurt, at the time Dean and Pastor of this parish.

Notes on the Latin
Matricula Baptizatorum — literally “Register of those baptized,” i.e., Baptismal Register.
Parochiæ Carolstadt — “of the parish of Karlstadt.”
incepta per — “begun by.”
Hassfurtensem — “the Hassfurt man,” meaning “from Hassfurt.”
p.t. — abbreviation of pro tempore (“at the time”).
Decanum Parochum hujatem — “Dean and Pastor of this parish.”
