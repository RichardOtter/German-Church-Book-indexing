Interpretation

oo.16.02.1845 Waldzell: Heinrich Schürger, * in Waldzell, legitimate son of Heinrich Schürger and his wife Margaretha, nee Mehling, married Maria Katharina Mehling, legitimate daughter of Heinrich Mehling and his wife Katharina, nee März (Diözesanarchiv Würzburg, Amtsbücher aus Pfarreien 5305, Fiche 3, p. 9).
