===========================================DIV50==

Overview

Generally, one book was used at a time for data entry.
It was subdivided into the categories of events.
When a new book was started, all new events went into the new book, although some dates overlap.

Later books had preprinted headers. (?)

Waldzell events from 1600-1700  (?) were recorded in the Steinfeld church book.
