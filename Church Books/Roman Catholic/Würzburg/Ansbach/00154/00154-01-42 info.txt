Translation
On 23 April, around the sixth hour of Vespers,
Johann Georg Sendelbach was baptized.
His father was Georg Adam Sendelbach, son of the most worthy and honorable Georg Sendelbach,
and his mother was Maria Catharina, née Mehling.
The sponsors were the honorable Johann Voscher and Michael Saletty, parishioners.
