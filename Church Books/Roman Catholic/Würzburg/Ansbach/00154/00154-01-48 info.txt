------------------------------------------------------------
3. LETTER-BY-LETTER ANALYSIS
------------------------------------------------------------

Hadim:
H-a-d-i-m. Non-Latin personal name; likely local or foreign.

obit / obiit:
o-b-i-t. Latin: “he/she died.”

hon:
h-o-n- : abbreviation for honestus (“respectable”).

Michal Lechent:
M-i-c-h-a-l (variant of Michael).
L-e-c-h-e-n-t (surname).

judicarius:
j-u-d-i-c-a-r-i-u-s = judicial officer.

Barbara nata Lepchetin:
n-a-t-a = “born,” maiden name indicator.
L-e-p-c-h-e-t-i-n = feminine Latin form of Lepchet.

ex Cabal:
C-a-b-a-l = place name.

obstetrix Raull:
o-b-s-t-e-t-r-i-x = midwife.
R-a-u-l-l = name as written.


------------------------------------------------------------
4. ENGLISH TRANSLATION (modern German names)
------------------------------------------------------------

On 31 January, at 11 PM,
Hadim died,
child of Michael Lechent, a judicial officer,
and Barbara Lepchet (née Lepchetin) from Cabal.

The midwife Raull is noted.
