Transcription
Die 4 to Jan circa ␤ 4 tam pomerid:
Nata eodem die. Baptisata est Elisabetha, filia legitima
honesti Joannis Georgii Mehling, molitoris, et Mariae
coniugis Joannis Ludovici Linde. Levans Elisabetha Ludovica
puella virgo. Baptizans Sachs.

On the 4th day of January, around 4 in the afternoon.
Born on the same day.
Elisabetha was baptized, the legitimate daughter of
the honorable Johann Georg Mehling, miller, and Maria,
daughter of Johann Ludwig Linde.
The one lifting (the child) was Elisabetha Ludovica,
an unmarried young woman.
The baptizing priest was Sachs.
