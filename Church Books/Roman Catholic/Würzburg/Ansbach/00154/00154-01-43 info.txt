Looking again at line 3:

`Möhlein ex° Waedgolsdula. baptiZav. Joann Anton Hersbach`

But more importantly — **the priest's name wouldn't be at the end of the record body anyway**. The typical format of these Catholic baptismal records is:

- Child's name
- Parents
- Godparent(s)
- **The officiant signed or was recorded separately**, often below or in a margin

So **"Joann Anton Hersbach"** (or similar) is most likely the **godfather** (*patrinus*), not the priest — and I was wrong to label him as the officiant. The record likely reads:

> godmother: **Anna Maria Möhlein** from **Waigolshausen**; godfather: **Johann Anton Hersbach**

And `baptiZav.` would then refer back to the act itself — "was baptized" — with no separate priest named in this line at all.
