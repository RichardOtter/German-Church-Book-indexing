Transcription
Die 25 junii circa 7 man
es gut machel.

Natus et eadem die Baptizatus est Joannes Michael, filius
legitimus Joannis Georgii Mehling et Anna Maria,
coniugum nata Brunnin. Levant Michael Mehling, Baptisma 
P. Longing cap.

Translation
On 25 June, around 7 in the morning.
(the 2nd line is a local note; see below)

Born and on the same day baptized was Johann Michael,
legitimate son of Johann Georg Mehling and Anna Maria,
a married couple, born Brunn.
Sponsors: Michael Mehling . Baptized by P. Longiny,
Chaplain.

Note: “es gut machel.”
This is vernacular German, very sloppy handwriting. 
This is likely a clerical note, not part of the baptism entry.
It may refer to preparing the register, correcting an entry, or performing a ritual “properly.”
