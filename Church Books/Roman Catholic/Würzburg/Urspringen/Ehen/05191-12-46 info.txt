Interpretation

by Thomas Wagner

2. Johann Schürger and his wife Anna Maria, nee Scheuermann:
oo.18.09.1825 Urspringen: Johann Schürger, *04.10.1795 in Urspringen, house number 107, legitimate son of the Johann Schürger and his wife Anna, nee Ebinger, married Anna Maria Scheuermann, *15.07.1797 in Urspringen, legitimate daughter of Stefan Scheuermann and his wife Dorothea, nee Handl, witnesses Matthäus Albert and Matthäus Scheuermann, both from Urspringen (Diözesanarchiv Würzburg, Amtsbücher aus Pfarreien 5191, Fiche 12, p. 9).


new as of 2023-08
oo.18.09.1825 Urspringen: Johann Schürger, *04.10.1798 in Urspringen, house number 107, legitimate son of the Johann Schürger and his wife Anna, nee Leininger (not Ebinger), married Anna Maria Scheuermann, *15.07.1797 in Urspringen, legitimate daughter of Stefan Scheuermann and his wife Dorothea, nee Handl, witnesses Matthäus Albert and Matthäus Scheuermann, both from Urspringen (Diözesanarchiv Würzburg, Amtsbücher aus Pfarreien 5191, Fiche 12, p. 9).
