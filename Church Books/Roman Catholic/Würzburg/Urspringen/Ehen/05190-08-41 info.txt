7. Johann Georg Schürger and his wife Maria Margaretha, nee Breitenbach:
oo03.02.1756 Urspringen: Johann Georg Schürger, legitimate son of the deceased Johann Georg Schürger from Urspringen and his wife Maria, married Margaretha Breitenbach, legitimate daughter of Michael Breitenbach from Wiesenfeld and his wife Barbara, witnesses Johann Michael Wiesner from Urspringen and Michael Brandt from Wiesenfeld (Diözesanarchiv Würzburg, Amtsbücher aus Pfarreien 5190, Fiche 8, p. 7).
