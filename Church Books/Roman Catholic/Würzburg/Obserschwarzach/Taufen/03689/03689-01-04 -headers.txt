===============================================================
English Translation

1. Name of the child
2. Type of birth dead or living, name of the midwife or the birth attendant
3. Name of the father and his surname
4. Status and religion of the father
5. Parish residence location and number of baptism
6. Name of the mother first and surname
7. Status of the mother and religion
8. District residence location and number of baptism
9. Time of birth day of the month year and hour
10. Day of baptism place of baptism district
11. Priest or his deputy
12. Godparents witnesses first and surname, status, residence
13. Deputy of the same first and surname status residence
