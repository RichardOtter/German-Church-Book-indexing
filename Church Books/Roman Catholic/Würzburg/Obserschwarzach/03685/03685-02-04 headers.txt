Looking at the first entry helps confirm the meanings:

Column 1 contains entries such as "Oberschwarzach die 25 Decem." (25 December, Oberschwarzach).
Column 2 has the child's name "Carolus".
Column 3 describes the parents.
Column 4 lists the godparent(s).
Column 5 contains birth details such as "die 6 Januarii circa 12 vespertinam" (6 January, about noon/afternoon).
Column 6 gives the baptizing priest, e.g. "Joannes Georgius Henricus".
Column 7 contains parish/status notes and legitimacy information.
Column 8 is for additional remarks.

A more literal reconstruction of the headings would be:

Mensis – Month/date
Baptizati – Baptized
Parentes – Parents
Patrini – Godparents
Domus, dies mensis, diei & hora nativitatis – House, day/date, and hour of birth
Baptizati – Baptizer
Parochia / Anno Domini 1804 – Parish record column
Observationes – Remarks
